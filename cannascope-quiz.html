What should I buy at the dispensary if I want to sleep better?

Look for strains high in myrcene and linalool — these terpenes are associated with sedation and relaxation. Indica-dominant strains like Granddaddy Purple, Northern Lights, or Wedding Cake are popular for sleep. Edibles taken 1–2 hours before bed give the longest-lasting effect. Start with 5mg THC and see how you feel.

I'm a beginner — what cannabis product should I get?

Start with a tincture or a low-dose edible (2.5–5mg THC). These are forgiving — you can dose precisely and the effects come on slowly. Avoid high-THC flower or concentrates your first time. A 1:1 THC:CBD ratio is also a great starting point since CBD tempers the intensity of THC.

What's the difference between indica, sativa, and hybrid?

The indica/sativa distinction is more about marketing than science — what actually determines effects are terpenes and cannabinoid ratios. That said: indica-leaning strains tend to be more body-focused and calming, sativa-leaning strains tend to be more cerebral and energizing, and hybrids fall in between.
