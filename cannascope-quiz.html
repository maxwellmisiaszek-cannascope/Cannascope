Questions people ask.

What's a Cannascope, exactly?

A blended cannabis identity profile. You answered 8 questions — Cannascope scored your responses across seven archetypes (Deep Sleeper, Sharpener, Healer, Social Spark, Unwinder, Explorer, Micro-Doser). Your card shows your primary archetype, your secondary tendencies, and translates those into specific strains, terpenes, and budtender language.

Why archetypes instead of just product recommendations?

Because the way you relate to cannabis is more stable than what's in stock today. A "Sleeper-leaning Explorer" wants different sleep strains than a pure "Sleeper" — and that pattern holds across dispensaries, seasons, and market shifts. We give you a portrait so you can adapt the recommendation yourself.

How accurate is this?

Accurate-feeling, not clinical. The science behind terpene-effect matching is real but not perfect — the same strain affects different people differently. Use your Cannascope as a starting framework, then refine it with your actual experience. Take it again any time your preferences shift.

Do I need an account?

No. Cannascope is anonymous unless you opt in to text or email. We don't require sign-up, we don't sell your data, and you can take the assessment as many times as you want.

Can I take this to my dispensary?

Yes — that's the whole point. Save the card as an image, screenshot it, or have us text it to you. Show it to your budtender. They'll see your archetype, recommended strains, terpenes, and the exact phrase to ask for.
