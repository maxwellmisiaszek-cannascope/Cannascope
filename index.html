FAQ

The questions every dispensary asks first.

How do I get the QR card?

We design it to your brand — your logo, store name, color palette — and either email you a print-ready PDF or ship the printed card directly to your store. Most stores are live within 5–7 days of signing up.

Do I need to train my staff?

No. The QR is customer-facing and the personality assessment runs entirely on the customer's own phone. Your budtenders never have to touch it. They just receive the customer's recommendation card and fill the order like normal.

Does this connect to my POS?

No. Cannascope is intentionally separate from your POS. We don't sync inventory, we don't read sales data, we don't touch your back office. We're a parallel customer experience layer that runs entirely on the counter QR.

What's actually in the monthly report?

Total assessments completed, archetype breakdown, goal mix (sleep / focus / pain / social / etc.), format preferences (flower / edible / vape / tincture), repeat visitor analysis, zip-code demand insights, and trend changes vs. the prior month. Delivered as a clean PDF you can forward to your team.

Who owns the customer data?

You do. The phone numbers, SMS consents, and assessment answers from customers who scan in your store are yours. We hold them on your behalf, but you can export the full list at any time, and we will delete them on request when you cancel.

Is the SMS opt-in actually compliant?

Yes — TCPA-compliant double opt-in, STOP/HELP keywords, registered 10DLC carrier campaign for cannabis-friendly delivery, full audit log per consent. We handle the legal side so you don't need to.
