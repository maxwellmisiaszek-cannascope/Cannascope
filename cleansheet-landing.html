FAQ

Questions, honestly answered.

Will an auditor actually accept CleanSheet evidence? +
Yes. Our policies are written to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (2017, current) and evidence is exported in standard PDF + document formats. We also partner with regional audit firms familiar with our output. Bring your own auditor if you'd rather — we'll make it easy.
Do you support Type 1 or Type 2? +
Both. Type 1 is a point-in-time attestation (typical first audit, 4–8 week prep) — what most customers use to unblock their first enterprise deal. Type 2 covers a 3–12 month observation window, usually the next year. CleanSheet supports both.
What about ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, PCI? +
SOC 2 only at launch. HIPAA overlap arrives Q3 2026. ISO 27001 follows. If you need multiple frameworks right now, Vanta is still the better call for now — we won't pretend otherwise.
Do I need to hire a security engineer? +
For SOC 2 Type 1, no — a technical founder or CTO can run the full process using CleanSheet. For Type 2 or highly sensitive data, you'll want a part-time vCISO (we'll refer one at $2–4k/mo if needed).
When does founding pricing end? +
When 25 founding members sign up. After that, standard pricing ($299 Starter, $599 Pro) applies to new customers. Founding members keep their $99 / $199 rate for 12 months from signup.
Is this built for a specific industry? +
Any B2B SaaS, agency, or ancillary tech company needing SOC 2 to close enterprise deals. We have particular depth in regulated industries — cannabis tech, fintech, and health tech — where compliance is existential.
What happens if I cancel? +
Export everything — policies as Word docs, evidence as a ZIP, reports as PDFs. Your data is yours. No lock-in, no data hostage.
